HOW TO PLAY

1. The Blueprint: Top area shows the final Colored Shapes you need to complete the level.

2. The Forge: Drag raw materials from the bottom into the empty slots in the middle to store them.

3. Crafting: Drag a Color onto a Shape inside the Forge to combine them into a Colored Shape.

4. Placement: Drag your finished Colored Shape up to the matching slot in the Blueprint.

5. Mistakes: Messed up? Drag unwanted items from the Forge into the Trash Can.
